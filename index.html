About the Series

Meet Charlie the cat — Kevin and Sami's trusty companion throughout the Adventure Island series!

Adventure Island is a complete ESL English series designed for young learners in Japan. Created by Graeme Cattermole, each book follows Kevin and Sami through a new world — from ocean to jungle, beach to snowy mountains, and beyond into enchanted skies.

Every book blends engaging storytelling with structured language learning — giving students the vocabulary, grammar, and confidence to communicate in real English.
